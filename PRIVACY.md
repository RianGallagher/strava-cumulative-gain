# Privacy Policy — Cumulative Elevation for Strava

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Cumulative Elevation for Strava ("the extension") adds elevation gain, loss and
optional climb details to Strava's own elevation charts. It is designed to
handle as little data as possible and to keep everything on your device.

## What the extension accesses

- **Strava pages you open.** The extension runs only on `www.strava.com`. It
  reads the elevation profile that Strava has already loaded to draw its chart.
- **Strava data for the page you are viewing.** On activity pages it requests
  the activity's distance, altitude and time streams from `www.strava.com`; on
  route pages it may request the route's GPX file from `www.strava.com`. These
  requests go only to Strava, using your existing Strava session, and return
  the same data Strava's page already shows you.

## What it stores

Two display preferences, kept in your browser's local storage for
`www.strava.com`: whether "Show climbs" is ticked, and whether the Splits
table shows net elevation or gain/loss. Nothing else is stored.

## What it does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no
  servers, analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data.
- It does not run on any site other than `www.strava.com`, and requests no
  browser permissions.

All calculations happen locally in your browser.
